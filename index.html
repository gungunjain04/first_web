Why This Site Exists

Many modern pages bury their content under auto-playing media, newsletter pop-ups, consent banners and piles of third-party scripts.

The web works better when pages are:

  • Clear
  • Accessible
  • Direct

This site is a small, friendly protest against over-engineering.

A Trip Back in Time

Landing here should feel like browsing in the mid-1990s:

  • No tricks to keep you scrolling.
  • No walls of pop-ups.
  • No tracking.
  • Only text, links and ideas.

If this page loads instantly and feels a little nostalgic, it is doing its job.

Our Principles

  • Simplicity: if something isn't needed, it isn't here.
  • Accessibility: content comes first, decoration comes second, or never.
  • Lightness: your browser and your data plan deserve respect.
  • Function over flash: a working idea beats a shiny toy.

What This Site Is

  • An experiment: how far can plain HTML take us?
  • A statement: a polite "no, thank you" to bloat.
  • A personal notebook: a place to write about technology, the web and anything else that fits.

What You Might Take Away

  • Curiosity: could you build something simpler?
  • A smile: yes, this really is only HTML.
  • A question: who should modern web standards serve, frameworks or people?

What Comes Next

More plain articles will appear here about technology, the web and related ideas. No fancy layout, no tracking, no fireworks. Just words and links.

Built with HTML only. No CSS. No JavaScript.
